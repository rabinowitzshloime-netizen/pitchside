Get the app

Put Pitchside on your phone

It's free and takes a few seconds. Pitchside then opens from your home screen like any other app.

iPhone or iPad (Safari)
  1. Open the Pitchside website in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
Android (Chrome)
  1. Open the Pitchside website in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
Computer (Chrome or Edge)
  1. Open the Pitchside website.
  2. Click the install icon at the right of the address bar.
  3. Click Install.
